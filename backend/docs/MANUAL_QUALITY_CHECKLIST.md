# Manual real-gameplay quality checklist

Use this only after a compatible standard QA report is release-ready. It supplements deterministic
QA; it does not change or overwrite the report.

- Does selected gameplay semantically match each narration requirement?
- Are the important gameplay moments visually clear rather than merely technically valid?
- Are cuts paced naturally for the narration?
- Are repeated clips or nearby repeated moments distracting?
- Is pronunciation acceptable in context?
- Does narration sound natural across independently synthesized blocks?
- Is the final video visually coherent from beginning to end?
- Are loading screens, menus, dark scenes or static moments editorially justified?

Record findings externally as an operational review. Stage 18 intentionally has no LLM reviewer,
automatic footage substitution, remaster, rerender or publishing action.
